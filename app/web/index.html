Fix the timing of the recordings

Between the band leaving the speakers and your guitar reaching the computer there is a small delay. This test measures it once; after that the app corrects every take by itself.

  1. Mute the strings with your fretting hand: resting on them, not pressing.
  2. Click Start. You will hear 8 clicks, a bit more than half a second apart.
  3. Strum the muted strings together with each click ("chk"), firmly.
